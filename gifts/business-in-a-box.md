# Business in a Box
### 10 mega-prompts for building full business systems from scratch

---

These aren't one-liner prompts. These are complete system-building prompts that produce everything you need to run a major part of your business — in one go.

Each mega-prompt produces a full working system, not just ideas. Paste it in, fill the brackets, and get something you can actually use.

---

## Mega-Prompt 1: Cold Outreach System

```
Build me a complete cold outreach system. I'll fill in the specifics:

My offer: [offer]
My avatar: [avatar — be specific: role, revenue/stage, location if relevant]
My price point: [price]
My biggest credibility proof: [e.g. "helped 12 coaches hit $10K months"]

Deliver all of the following:

1. ICP Definition — describe exactly who I should be targeting. Include: job title or role, company/business type, revenue or stage, pain points they're experiencing, buying signals that show they're ready.

2. 3 Outreach Channels — pick the 3 best channels for my ICP with a brief rationale for each (e.g. Instagram DMs, LinkedIn, cold email).

3. Message Templates — for each channel, write:
   - First message (conversation opener, no pitch)
   - Follow-up 1 (if no reply after 2–3 days)
   - Follow-up 2 (if still no reply after another 3 days)
   - Closing message (final attempt, low pressure)

4. Tracking Spreadsheet Structure — give me the column headers and dropdown options for a simple outreach tracker. Columns should track: name, channel, contact date, stage (new/contacted/replied/booked/closed/lost), notes.

Format everything clearly so I can hand it to a VA or start using it today.
```

## Mega-Prompt 2: Full Sales Process

```
Design my full sales process from first touch to closed deal.

My offer: [offer]
My price point: [price]
My sales call format: [e.g. "30-minute Zoom discovery call"]
My biggest objections: [list 2–3 common objections you hear]

Deliver all of the following:

1. Pipeline Stages — name each stage and define exactly what it means for a lead to be at that stage (not vague — specific criteria).

2. What Happens at Each Stage — for each stage, describe: what action needs to happen, who does it (me or automated), and what the goal is before moving to the next stage.

3. Messages to Send — write the actual messages (text, WhatsApp, or email) for:
   - Lead just booked a call
   - Reminder 24 hours before the call
   - Reminder 1 hour before the call
   - No-show recovery (within 2 hours)
   - Post-call follow-up (didn't close — needs follow-up)
   - Closed! Welcome and next steps

4. Sales Call Script — write a complete outline for my sales call:
   - Opener and rapport (2 minutes)
   - Discovery questions (5–7 questions to uncover pain and urgency)
   - Pitch structure (outcome, proof, what's included, price)
   - Objection handling for my specific objections
   - Close script (exact words to ask for the yes)

Format as a step-by-step playbook I can follow and hand to any closer.
```

## Mega-Prompt 3: 30-Day Content Calendar

```
Create a 30-day content calendar for [platform: e.g. Instagram].

My offer: [offer]
My avatar: [avatar]
My voice: [e.g. "direct, no-fluff, real stories, Hormozi-influenced"]
My content goal: [e.g. "build authority and drive DMs from warm leads"]

For each of the 30 days, provide:
- Day number and date (start from [date: e.g. May 5])
- Content format (e.g. talking head, B-roll, carousel, story, text post)
- Hook — the first line or on-screen text. Scroll-stopping.
- Core message — what's the one thing this post says or teaches?
- CTA — what do you want them to do after watching/reading?

Content mix across the 30 days:
- 10 educational/value posts (teach something specific)
- 8 story/personal posts (real moments, lessons learned)
- 5 social proof/results posts (case studies, transformations, wins)
- 4 direct offer/CTA posts (clear asks)
- 3 behind-the-scenes posts (show the process or day-to-day)

Format as a clean table or numbered list so I can load this into a scheduler.
```

## Mega-Prompt 4: Client Onboarding System

```
Build a complete client onboarding system for my [service/program: e.g. "90-day 1:1 high-ticket sales coaching"].

My avatar: [avatar]
Program length: [length]
What I deliver: [brief overview of deliverables]
How we communicate: [e.g. "weekly Zoom calls + WhatsApp between calls"]

Deliver all of the following:

1. Welcome Sequence — 3 messages:
   - Message 1 (immediately after signing): make them feel they made the right call, set expectations
   - Message 2 (day 2): practical next steps, what to prepare
   - Message 3 (day before kickoff call): excitement, confirmation, what to expect on the call

2. Kickoff Call Agenda — a complete run-of-show for the first call:
   - How to open (make them feel at ease)
   - What to cover (goals, situation, expectations, communication norms)
   - What to assign before the next call
   - How to close the call

3. Weeks 1–4 Delivery Milestones — for each week:
   - What the client should accomplish
   - What I deliver or facilitate
   - Key check-in moment (when and how)

4. Check-In Cadence — for the full program:
   - When are the scheduled touchpoints?
   - What does each touchpoint look like?
   - What's the early warning sign they're disengaging, and what do I do?

Format as a complete onboarding playbook.
```

## Mega-Prompt 5: Referral System

```
Create a complete referral system for my business.

My offer: [offer]
My typical client result: [e.g. "coaches hit $10K months within 90 days"]
My ideal referral source: [e.g. "happy clients who've gotten results"]

Deliver all of the following:

1. Who to Ask — define the ideal referral partner. What results should they have gotten? How long should they have been a client? Any other criteria?

2. When to Ask — at what specific moments in the client journey is it natural to ask for a referral? List 3–5 trigger moments (e.g. "after they hit their first big win," "at the 30-day mark").

3. What to Say — write 3 versions of the referral ask:
   - Casual WhatsApp/text version
   - Email version
   - On a call (script for how to bring it up naturally)

4. How to Make It Easy — give the client ready-to-use language they can copy-paste when referring. Write 2 versions: one for DMs, one for a verbal referral.

5. Referral Reward Structure — give me 3 options for how to reward referrals (cash, discount, upgrade, recognition) with a brief pro/con for each. I'll pick the one that fits.

6. Follow-Up if They Don't Refer — if a client said they'd refer but nothing came through after 2 weeks, write a gentle follow-up message.

Format as a complete referral playbook I can start using this week.
```

## Mega-Prompt 6: Lead Magnet System

```
Design my lead magnet from scratch.

My offer: [offer — what do people buy from me?]
My avatar: [avatar — who am I trying to attract?]
My biggest competitor's lead magnet: [describe if you know, or say "unknown"]
My biggest credibility point: [your best result or proof]

Deliver all of the following:

1. Lead Magnet Concept — recommend the best format (checklist, guide, video, calculator, template, etc.) with a brief rationale based on my avatar.

2. Title — write 3 headline options for the lead magnet. Each should promise a specific outcome and speak directly to the avatar's pain or desire.

3. 5 Key Sections — what are the 5 sections or chapters of the lead magnet? For each: section title + 2 sentences on what it covers.

4. 3-Email Follow-Up Sequence — after they download, send 3 emails that build trust and sell the next step:
   - Email 1 (immediate): deliver the resource, introduce yourself, set up what's coming
   - Email 2 (day 2): one genuinely useful tip or insight, build authority
   - Email 3 (day 4): soft pitch for the next step (the offer), with a clear CTA

For each email: subject line, preview text, body, CTA.

Format as a complete lead magnet brief + email sequence I can hand to a copywriter or use directly.
```

## Mega-Prompt 7: Testimonial Collection System

```
Build my testimonial collection system.

My offer: [offer]
My typical client result: [best result you've gotten for a client]
Where I use testimonials: [e.g. "sales page, IG, DMs, proposals"]

Deliver all of the following:

1. When to Ask — list the 3–5 best moments in the client journey to ask for a testimonial (specific, not generic). Include what trigger indicates it's the right time.

2. How to Ask — write 3 versions of the ask:
   - WhatsApp/text (casual)
   - Email (slightly more formal)
   - On a call (how to bring it up naturally before ending)

3. What Questions to Ask — write 5 specific questions that pull out a powerful testimonial. Questions should produce: before/after transformation, specific result, who this is for, and an endorsement of me specifically.

4. Formatting for Max Social Proof — once I have the testimonial, how should I format it? Give me a template for: IG graphic text, sales page quote, DM use (copy-paste-ready).

5. Video Testimonial Brief — write a simple brief I can send to clients who are willing to record a video. Include: what to say, length, technical setup (lighting, camera tips), and how to send it to me.

Format as a system I can start using immediately, with all copy ready to go.
```

## Mega-Prompt 8: 90-Day Client Retention Plan

```
Create a 90-day client retention plan for my [service/program].

My offer: [offer]
Program length: [e.g. "3 months"]
Typical churn reason: [why do clients disengage or not renew? e.g. "results slow in month 2, life gets in the way"]
My communication channel: [e.g. "WhatsApp + Zoom calls"]

Deliver all of the following:

1. Touchpoint Calendar — map out every intentional touchpoint across 90 days:
   - Scheduled calls (when, what's covered)
   - Check-in messages (when, what to say)
   - Value drops (bonus resources, wins shared, encouragement)
   - Milestone moments (when to celebrate)

2. Value Delivery Moments — identify 5 specific moments across the 90 days where you can deliver unexpected value (a resource, a shoutout, a check-in that goes above expectations).

3. Early Warning Signs — list the top 5 signals a client is about to churn or disengage. For each signal, write a specific action I should take when I see it.

4. Re-Engagement Script — write a message for when a client has gone quiet for 5+ days. Goal: reconnect without making them feel guilty.

5. Renewal Approach — write a message and a brief pitch for renewing or upgrading a client at the end of the 90 days. Frame it as a natural next step, not a sales call.

Format as a complete retention playbook I can follow for every client.
```

## Mega-Prompt 9: Meta Ads System

```
Build my Meta Ads system from scratch.

My offer: [offer]
My avatar: [avatar — as specific as possible: age, gender, interests, behaviors, pain]
My budget: [monthly budget in USD]
My goal: [e.g. "book 15 sales calls per month"]
My landing page / funnel: [describe what happens after the click]

Deliver all of the following:

1. Campaign Structure — what campaigns should I run and in what order? (e.g. TOF → MOF → BOF, or testing → scaling). Name each campaign and explain its purpose.

2. Audience Layers — for each campaign, define:
   - Cold audiences (interest stacks, lookalikes, or broad with signals)
   - Warm retargeting audiences (page visitors, video views, lead list)
   - Custom audience settings

3. Ad Formats — what formats should I use for each campaign level? (video, image, carousel, story). Give a brief rationale for each choice.

4. Budget Allocation — how should I split the monthly budget across campaigns? Give me percentages and a brief rationale.

5. Optimization Rules — write 5 specific rules for when and how to optimize:
   - When to kill an ad (specific metric thresholds)
   - When to scale a winner (specific metric thresholds)
   - What to do when CPL spikes
   - How often to refresh creatives
   - Weekly review checklist

Format as a complete ads playbook I can follow or hand to a media buyer.
```

## Mega-Prompt 10: Monday Morning Review System

```
Design my Monday morning review system.

My business: [describe: e.g. "high-ticket coaching for agency owners, 7 active clients, 20 leads in pipeline, running Meta ads, posting content 5x/week"]
My goals this month: [list 2–3 specific goals]
My tools: [e.g. "GHL, Meta Ads Manager, Google Sheets, Instagram"]
Time I have for this review: [e.g. "30 minutes"]

Deliver all of the following:

1. Metrics to Check — list the exact metrics I should review every Monday, organized by category (leads, sales, clients, content, ads). For each metric: what it is, where to find it, and what a good vs. bad number looks like.

2. Decision Framework — if numbers are off, what do I do? Write a simple if/then decision tree:
   - If leads are down → do X
   - If CPL is up → do Y
   - If a client is at risk → do Z
   (customize to my business)

3. Weekly Priority Framework — how do I pick my top 3 priorities for the week? Write a simple process (not a complex system — something I'll actually do in 5 minutes).

4. Monday Review Prompt — write a complete Claude Code prompt I can paste every Monday morning. This prompt should: ask me for my current numbers, surface what needs attention, help me set my top 3 priorities, and draft any urgent messages I need to send. Make it ready to use as-is.

5. 15-Minute Version — if I only have 15 minutes, what's the minimum viable Monday review? Give me the 5-question checklist version.

Format as a complete Monday operating system I can start using this week.
```
