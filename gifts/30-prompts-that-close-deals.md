# 30 Prompts That Close Deals
### Copy-paste Claude Code prompts for coaches, consultants, agency owners, and insurance directors

---

> **How to use these:** Copy the prompt, paste it into Claude Code, fill in the [brackets], hit enter. Each prompt is designed to produce output you can use immediately — no editing required (though you should always review before sending).

---

## Lead Generation

**Prompt 1 — Cold DM**
```
Write a cold DM targeting [avatar: e.g. "life coaches with 1,000–10,000 Instagram followers"] about [pain point: e.g. "not converting followers into paying clients"]. My offer is [offer: e.g. "a 90-day high-ticket sales coaching program"]. Keep it under 5 lines. No pitch in the first message — the goal is to start a conversation. Make it feel like a real person sent it, not a template.
```

**Prompt 2 — Meta Ad Hooks**
```
Create 5 ad hook variations for [offer: e.g. "a recruitment system for insurance directors"], targeting [audience: e.g. "life insurance agents in Malaysia aged 25–40"] on Meta. Use these hook types: one bold claim, one question, one counterintuitive statement, one "if you're X then this is for you", one pain callout. Keep each hook under 2 lines.
```

**Prompt 3 — LinkedIn Outreach Sequence**
```
Write a 3-step LinkedIn outreach sequence targeting [ICP: e.g. "founders of 7-figure coaching businesses"]. Step 1: connection request note (under 300 characters). Step 2: follow-up after they connect (start a conversation, no pitch). Step 3: soft transition to a call if they're engaging. Match the tone to someone who is direct, credible, and not desperate.
```

**Prompt 4 — VSL Headlines**
```
Generate 10 headline variations for a VSL targeting [avatar: e.g. "agency owners who are stuck under $20K/month"]. Each headline should create curiosity or call out a specific pain. Avoid hype words. Make them feel like something a smart, straight-talking founder would say — not a marketer.
```

**Prompt 5 — Webinar Title + Bullets**
```
Write a webinar title and 3 bullet points for a webinar about [topic: e.g. "how to recruit 5 insurance agents a month without chasing people"] targeting [ICP: e.g. "insurance directors who are tired of warm-market recruiting"]. The title should be specific and promise a clear outcome. Each bullet should tease a specific thing they'll learn without giving it away.
```

## Sales & Closing

**Prompt 6 — No-Show Follow-Up**
```
Write a follow-up text for a lead who missed their sales call with me today. My offer is [offer]. Make it short, warm (not passive-aggressive), and easy for them to rebook. Give me 2 versions: one for same day, one for 24 hours later.
```

**Prompt 7 — Objection Handling: "I Need to Think About It"**
```
Write 3 objection-handling responses for when a prospect says "I need to think about it" on a sales call for [offer: e.g. "a $3,000/month coaching program"]. Each response should: acknowledge without agreeing, uncover what's really holding them back, and move toward a decision. Keep them conversational — not scripted. My audience is [avatar].
```

**Prompt 8 — Re-Engagement DM**
```
Write a re-engagement DM for a lead who went cold [X: 30] days ago. They originally showed interest in [offer]. Don't mention that they went cold or make them feel bad. The goal is to restart the conversation naturally. Give me 2 versions: one direct, one story-based.
```

**Prompt 9 — Case Study in Story Format**
```
Write a case study breakdown of this client result in story format: [paste result: e.g. "Client was a life coach struggling to get 2 clients/month. After 8 weeks, they hit 6 clients at $2,500 each = $15K month"]. Structure: Before (their situation and pain) → The turning point (what changed) → After (specific result) → Quote or lesson. Keep it under 250 words. Make it feel real, not polished PR.
```

**Prompt 10 — Sales Page Section**
```
Write a sales page section (hero + offer + guarantee) for [offer: e.g. "a 90-day 1:1 coaching program for agency owners"]. My avatar is [avatar]. The hero should call out the person and promise the outcome. The offer section should list what's included in plain language. The guarantee should reduce risk and sound confident — not desperate. My price point is [price].
```

## Client Delivery

**Prompt 11 — Weekly Check-In Message**
```
Write a weekly check-in message to send to all my active clients. I want it to feel personal, not like a mass blast. It should: acknowledge where they are in the program, ask one specific question to keep momentum, and remind them of any upcoming deadlines or calls. My program is [program name/type: e.g. "a 90-day sales coaching program"].
```

**Prompt 12 — Onboarding Welcome Sequence**
```
Write an onboarding welcome sequence (3 messages) for new clients who just signed up for [offer]. Message 1: sent immediately after signing — make them feel they made the right decision, set expectations for week 1. Message 2: sent day 2 — practical next steps and what to prepare for the kickoff call. Message 3: sent day before kickoff call — pump them up and confirm the call details. Keep each message under 150 words.
```

**Prompt 13 — 30-Day Testimonial Request**
```
Write a 30-day review email asking for a testimonial from a client in [program/service type]. Make it feel natural — not like a corporate survey. Ask 3 specific questions that will produce a useful testimonial: one about where they were before, one about a specific result, one about who they'd recommend this to. Include a line that makes it easy to say yes.
```

**Prompt 14 — Client Progress Report Template**
```
Create a client progress report template for [service type: e.g. "a lead generation retainer for coaches"]. Include sections for: period covered, key metrics this period, wins to highlight, areas to improve, focus for next period, and any decisions needed from the client. Make it skimmable — they should be able to read it in 2 minutes.
```

**Prompt 15 — Retention Offer**
```
Write a retention offer message for a client whose contract is up next month. My service is [service]. The goal is to renew before they start thinking about leaving. Include: an acknowledgment of their progress, what's possible in the next period, the renewal offer or incentive (I'll fill in the details), and a soft deadline. Make it feel like an exciting next step, not a renewal notice.
```

## Content & Positioning

**Prompt 16 — Weekly Instagram Captions**
```
Write 7 Instagram captions for the week based on my offer [offer] and avatar [avatar]. Mix of formats: 2 educational, 2 story-based, 1 social proof/results, 1 behind the scenes, 1 direct CTA post. Each caption must have: a scroll-stopping first line (the hook), a body that delivers value or story, and a CTA at the top (above the fold). My voice is [describe voice: e.g. "direct, Malaysian, Hormozi-style, no fluff"].
```

**Prompt 17 — Day in the Life Reel Script**
```
Write a "day in the life" Reel script for [niche: e.g. "a high-ticket sales coach"]. Hook (0–3 seconds) should make it feel aspirational or relatable. Show real moments — not just highlights. Include at least one honest moment (a challenge or something imperfect). End with a message that positions the person as someone worth following. Script should be 45–60 seconds when spoken at a natural pace.
```

**Prompt 18 — 10 Authority Content Ideas**
```
Generate 10 content ideas that position me as an authority in [niche]. My offer is [offer], my avatar is [avatar]. Mix of formats: talking head, B-roll, before/after, hot take, myth-bust, educational, and case study. Each idea should include: the hook line, the core message, and the content format. Prioritize topics that my ideal clients are already searching for or talking about.
```

**Prompt 19 — LinkedIn Thought Leadership Post**
```
Write a LinkedIn thought-leadership post about [topic] in my voice. My voice is [describe: e.g. "direct, no-BS, Hormozi-influenced, not corporate"]. The post should: open with a specific insight or counterintuitive claim, tell a short story or share a real experience that backs it up, end with a lesson or point of view. Length: 150–250 words. No hashtag spam — max 2.
```

**Prompt 20 — YouTube Title, Thumbnail Text, Hook**
```
Write a YouTube title, thumbnail text, and opening hook for a video about [topic] targeting [avatar]. Title should be SEO-friendly and click-worthy — not misleading. Thumbnail text should be 3–5 words that make someone stop scrolling. Opening hook (first 30 seconds of the video) should state what they'll learn, why it matters to them, and what happens if they don't. Give me 3 variations of each.
```

## Automation & Operations

**Prompt 21 — GHL Workflow Description**
```
Write a GHL workflow description for what should happen when a lead books a call through my funnel. My funnel is [describe: e.g. "Meta ad → landing page → Cal.com booking"]. Include: trigger, immediate confirmation message, reminder sequence (1 day before, 1 hour before), what happens if they no-show, and what happens after the call depending on outcome (closed, follow-up needed, not a fit). Write it in plain language so I can hand this to a VA to build.
```

**Prompt 22 — Pipeline Follow-Up SMS**
```
Write 5 follow-up SMS messages for leads at different pipeline stages. Stage 1: Just booked a call (confirmation). Stage 2: No-show (re-engage within 2 hours). Stage 3: Call happened, didn't close — needs follow-up. Stage 4: Said "I need to think about it" — follow up 48 hours later. Stage 5: Cold lead, 2+ weeks no reply — last attempt to re-engage. Each message should be under 160 characters and feel like a real person sent it.
```

**Prompt 23 — Monday Morning Review SOP**
```
Write an SOP for how I want Claude to handle my Monday morning review. Every Monday I want to: review what happened last week, prioritize the top 3 things for this week, check on leads in my pipeline, and know if anything is falling through the cracks. My business is [describe: e.g. "a high-ticket coaching business with 8 active clients and a live pipeline of 15 leads"]. Write the SOP as a Claude Code prompt I can paste every Monday.
```

**Prompt 24 — Referral Request Message**
```
Draft a referral request message for happy clients. I want to send this to [X] clients who've gotten good results. My offer is [offer]. The message should: acknowledge their result specifically (I'll personalize), explain what kind of person would benefit from what I do, make it easy to refer (give them language), and offer something if they refer (I'll fill in the incentive). Keep it warm and direct — not salesy.
```

**Prompt 25 — CRM Re-Activation Campaign**
```
Write a re-activation campaign (3 messages) for dead leads in my CRM who haven't responded in 60+ days. My offer is [offer]. Message 1: Pattern interrupt — something unexpected that restarts the conversation. Message 2 (3 days later if no reply): A new angle or relevant piece of value. Message 3 (5 days later if no reply): Final message — low-pressure close or opt-out. Each message should be under 100 words and feel human.
```

## Insurance Recruitment (Bonus Section)

**Prompt 26 — Agent Recruiting DM**
```
Write a recruiting DM targeting life insurance agents in [city/country]. They're currently licensed but may be at a company with low support, bad culture, or capped income potential. My value proposition is [e.g. "high payout, mentorship, and a proven lead system"]. Keep it under 5 lines. Goal is to open a conversation — not explain everything in one message. Make it feel peer-to-peer, not a job posting.
```

**Prompt 27 — Objection Handling: "I'm Happy Where I Am"**
```
Write 3 objection responses for when a recruited agent says "I'm happy where I am." I'm an insurance director recruiting quality agents to my team. My team offers [brief differentiator]. Each response should: acknowledge their position, open a genuine question to understand their situation, and plant a seed without being pushy. Don't sound desperate. Keep each response under 5 lines.
```

**Prompt 28 — Recruiting Landing Page Copy**
```
Write a recruiting landing page headline, subheadline, and 3 bullets for my insurance director recruiting page. My target is [e.g. "licensed life insurance agents who want to scale their income past $10K/month without chasing friends and family"]. The headline should call out the person and promise an outcome. Subheadline supports and adds credibility. Bullets should each address a specific objection or desire. No corporate language.
```

**Prompt 29 — Agent Lead Follow-Up Sequence**
```
Write a follow-up sequence for agent leads who downloaded my [resource: e.g. "free income calculator / recruiting guide"]. 3 messages total. Message 1: Immediate — thank them, deliver the resource, introduce yourself. Message 2: Day 2 — add value, ask a genuine question about their situation. Message 3: Day 4 — soft invitation to a 15-minute chat, no pressure. Make them feel like they're talking to a leader who gives a damn, not a recruiter with a quota.
```

**Prompt 30 — "Why Join My Team" Section**
```
Write a "why join my team" section for my insurance director recruiting page. I need this to feel authentic and specific — not generic corporate copy. My team offers: [list your real advantages: e.g. "weekly training, mentorship from someone doing $X/month, lead generation support, high payout structure"]. Write it from my POV as the director. Include: what makes us different, what agents on my team actually experience, and who this is NOT for (add credibility by being selective).
```
