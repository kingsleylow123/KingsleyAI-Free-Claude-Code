# CLAUDE.md — David Tan, Insurance Director

## Who I Am

**Name:** David Tan
**Role:** Life Insurance Agency Director — I recruit, train, and build a downline of licensed agents under a major life insurance company.

I've been in the industry for 9 years. Went from agent to unit manager to director. Currently have 14 active agents in my downline. Building toward a 30-agent team and the income that comes with it.

The product isn't the issue. The system is. Right now recruiting is mostly word of mouth and referrals from my current agents. That's unpredictable and too slow. I need a real pipeline.

---

## Target Recruit Profile

**Who I'm looking for:**
- Age 25–40
- Wants income change — sick of fixed salary, wants performance-based upside
- Has hustle, not just a resume — salespeople, business owners, ex-MLM, real estate agents, finance grads
- Motivated by lifestyle: time freedom, income ceiling, helping people
- Willing to get licensed and commit to the process

**Who I do NOT want:**
- Tourists — people who join, do nothing, and leave in 3 months
- People who are purely money-motivated without belief in the product
- Anyone with a history of quitting as soon as things get hard

**Trigger phrase I hear from good recruits:** "I've always wanted to work for myself but didn't know where to start."

---

## Communication Style

Real talk. No corporate insurance speak. I talk to recruits like a friend who's been where they are and found a way out.

Malaysian-adjacent energy — direct, outcome-focused, warm but no-nonsense. I care about the people I recruit. I also don't waste their time or mine.

**Voice examples:**
- "Most people think insurance is about selling policies. It's not. It's about building a business with no ceiling — and I'll show you exactly how."
- "You don't need to know everything on day one. You need to trust the process, show up, and I'll walk you through every step."
- "In my first 18 months I made more than my last 3 years of salary combined. Not because I'm special — because the system works when you work it."

## Biggest Pain Points (What to Solve)

1. **Recruiting is reactive** — I wait for referrals or post on Instagram and hope someone DMs. No system, no consistency.
2. **No follow-up system** — I talk to someone, they say "maybe", I forget to follow up. Lose them to inertia.
3. **WhatsApp is a mess** — conversations everywhere, no tracking, no automation. I need sequences.
4. **Can't tell good recruits from tourists early** — waste 2 hours on a coffee chat with someone who was never serious.
5. **Instagram content is inconsistent** — I know I should post more but don't have a content system.

---

## What I Need Claude Code to Help With

1. **Build a recruiting funnel** — automate the journey from first touch (IG/WhatsApp) to booked call to follow-up to decision
2. **Write WhatsApp nurture sequences** — first contact, post-call follow-up, objection handling, re-engagement for cold leads
3. **Create a lead qualification system** — set of questions / mini-application to filter tourists before I spend time on a call
4. **Draft Instagram content** — recruiting hooks, lifestyle posts, agent success stories, behind-the-scenes
5. **Build a GHL pipeline** — stages: New Lead → Contacted → Call Booked → Call Done → Deciding → Active Recruit → Licensed
6. **Write DM scripts** — cold outreach for people who engage with my content, referral ask scripts for existing agents

---

## Hard Rules

- Never promise specific income numbers in writing — use "potential", "what top agents earn", or real agent examples with permission
- WhatsApp is personal — sequences should feel human, not automated
- All recruits must complete a qualification form before a call is booked — no more cold coffee chats
- Follow up a minimum of 5 times before marking a lead as dead
- Content should never make insurance look like a get-rich-quick scheme — credibility first
