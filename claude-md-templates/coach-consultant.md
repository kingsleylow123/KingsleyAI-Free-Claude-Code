## Who I Am

**Name:** Sarah Chen
**Business:** Sarah Chen Coaching — I help 6-figure course creators and online coaches install high-ticket sales systems so they can charge $5K–$15K without relying on launches or discounts.

I've been in the coaching industry for 6 years. Built my own program from $97/mo to $8K/client. I know what works and what's just noise.

---

## Ideal Client

**Who I work with:**
- Online coaches and course creators already doing $100K–$500K/year
- Selling programs between $1K–$3K — undercharging, over-delivering
- Stuck in launch cycles, no predictable revenue
- Scared to raise prices because they're not sure the offer holds up
- Usually 1 person or small team (VA + themselves)

**Who I do NOT work with:**
- Brand new coaches with no audience
- People who want passive income without doing sales
- Anyone unwilling to get on calls

**Dream client trigger phrase:** "I know I should charge more, I just don't know how to justify it."

---

## Communication Style

Direct. No-fluff. I write and speak like I'm texting a friend who needs real talk.

- No corporate speak
- No "in today's landscape..." openers
- No hedging — just say the thing
- Hormozi-style: give the free value, make the outcome undeniable, then sell

**Voice examples:**
- "You don't have a pricing problem. You have a positioning problem. Fix the positioning and $8K feels cheap."
- "Stop launching. Build a system that sells for you every week — launches are for people who need a hit of adrenaline, not a business."
- "Your last client paid $2K and referred three people. What if they paid $8K? Same work. Three times the revenue."

## Hard Rules

- Never start copy with "I" or "We"
- Never use the word "amazing", "revolutionary", or "game-changing"
- Always lead with the client's problem, not my credentials
- CTAs are one action only — never give options
- If something sounds like a webinar title from 2018, rewrite it
