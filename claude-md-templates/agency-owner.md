- Has tried ads before, got burned, needs someone who actually delivers

**Verticals we're good at:** financial services, professional services, aesthetics/med spa, coaching (but high-ticket only)

**Red flags:** e-commerce, one-person shows with no team, anyone who says "just run some Facebook ads"

## Communication Style

Straight talk. I'm not here to coddle. My clients pay $2K–$5K/month and they want numbers, not updates.

Internal docs and team communication: concise, structured, bulleted.
Client-facing copy: professional but direct — no agency jargon, no buzzwords.

**Voice examples:**
- "Your CPL went from $42 to $31. Here's what changed and what we're doing next week."
- "This campaign isn't underperforming — the offer is. Let's talk about what we're sending traffic to."
- "We run three ad variations, pick the winner at day 7, cut the losers, scale the survivor. That's the system."

## Biggest Pain Points (What to Solve)

1. **Client reporting is manual and takes too long** — I spend 6+ hours/week pulling numbers and writing updates. Need automated reports that pull from Meta and GHL and format into a client-ready doc.
2. **Campaign setup is repetitive** — Every new client needs the same landing page, same GHL pipeline, same initial ad structure. Should be templated and faster.
3. **Follow-up with leads is inconsistent** — Clients don't follow up fast enough. Need stronger GHL automation sequences built for each vertical.
4. **Onboarding new clients is chaos** — No standardized intake process, strategy call is unstructured. Need a system.
5. **Client communication is reactive** — I'm answering the same questions weekly. Need templated responses and a client-facing FAQ hub.

## Hard Rules

- Reports go to clients on the 1st of every month, no exceptions
- Never promise a specific CPL or lead count in writing — always use "target" or "benchmark"
